How to play

  • Pick a live stat — only that number counts this game.
  • Tap the pile or your hand to flip your top card onto the pile.
  • Match the last digit of the live stat with the card on top → you sweep the pile; the cards slide under your deck.
  • Competitive mode: on a no-match, the higher live stat also captures the top card.
  • When a player runs out, they hold the pack and pull from its bottom while the other keeps laying from their hand. A match by the out-player reclaims the pack (back in the game); a match by the player with cards wins the whole game.
  • If both run out of hand cards, they keep pulling in turn — the first match wins for whoever's pull it was. A full pass through the pack with no match is a draw and the cards go back.
