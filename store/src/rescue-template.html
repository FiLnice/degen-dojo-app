How to read the result

Every finding is sorted into three buckets:

Stuck — held by a program or in its vault. This is what a recovery targets; the program ID is your starting point.

Needs review — a program is involved but ambiguous (a delegate can be a staking lock or a marketplace listing). Check the mint on Solscan first.

Cleared — frozen but not staked (pNFTs are frozen by design). Nothing to undo.

Detection is heuristic — always confirm the program ID on Solscan. Found something stuck? Bring it to our Discord.
