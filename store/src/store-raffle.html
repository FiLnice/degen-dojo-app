🎁 Add to the prize

Give SOL, ORE, any token or NFTs to round –’s prize — a community can sponsor the week. No tickets: 100% goes to the winners. SOL and ORE grow the ORE prize (and can unlock more winners); other tokens are split like the prize; NFTs go one per winner, 1st place first. Verified items show right away, anything else after a quick review.
Burning is permanent. Every transaction is built in your browser and simulated before your wallet sees it — you approve it yourself, and nothing is sent without that. Each one burns your ticked NFTs and sends exactly the rent they free to the Dojo Treasury (shamanchef.sol) with a “ddraffle” memo; your tickets are counted from that transaction on-chain. Sacrifice a Degen? Allowed — every card burned makes the rest scarcer (Degen Dojo, Demon Dojo and TETO alike). You confirm it twice. DeFi positions and staked, frozen or listed NFTs are never offered. Gifts go to the same wallet with a “ddpot” memo and never give tickets. The prize’s SOL is swapped to ORE when the winners are paid. Draw records: latest round.
