Degen Dojo

CLUCK-FU

🐔 Choose your Chicken, first past the line wins.
Your bet joins that bird's pool — the more that's bet on a bird, the smaller each winner's cut.

🐔 How to play

  • Choose a bird by tapping its row.
  • Backing a bird costs 1 coin per race.
  • Hit Start the scramble; first past the line wins.
  • Winners split 7/8 of the pot by how much each backer put in.
  • The last 1/8 feeds the weekly Dojo Pot.
  • Provably-fair from a future block.
