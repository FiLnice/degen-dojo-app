Burn a card

Every Mint card's burn value is an equal share of its collection's Card Vault (88% of every royalty and repair across the collection) — a Framed card counts as two shares — paid in stORE. Cracked or Mono cards must be repaired to Mint first. Burn the card and that share is yours — paid in the next weekly payout.
⚠ Burning is permanent. The card is destroyed forever — it can’t be restored, sold or played again. Only burn a card you’re sure about.
How to burn a card (no wallet connection needed)
  1. Open the card in Phantom (or your wallet) → Send.
  2. Send it to shamanchef.sol — the Degen Dojo Treasury:
  3. Send. Its share of the Card Vault is locked the moment it arrives and paid to the wallet that sent it in the next weekly payout, in stORE. The Dojo then burns the card.
Prefer to burn it yourself? Connect your wallet (top right) and use “Burn it myself” on a card — one signature, the card is burned on-chain straight away.
